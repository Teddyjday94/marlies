Our Table

A local table with a familiar feel.

Marlie's opened in French Settlement in 2023 with Louisiana seafood, steaks, gumbo and a dining room meant for regulars, families and anybody passing through.

Since 2023

Opened in French Settlement.

Marlie's opened its doors on March 1, 2023. The public opening announcement introduced Micah and Cory as partners. Since then, the restaurant has become a local stop for seafood, steaks, gumbo and weekday specials.

The menu fits the area: seafood, gumbo, fried platters, steaks, pasta, po'boys and weekly specials.

The room matters, too

Casual, comfortable and local.

The dining room mixes wood, a neighborhood bar, floral touches and Marlie's own black-and-white branding. It feels polished enough for a night out and relaxed enough for a weeknight dinner.

“Good food. Good people. Come sit awhile.”

That same feeling carries through the restaurant: good food, a comfortable room and people coming back to the same table.
